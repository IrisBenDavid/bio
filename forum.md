## Forum on Economic Aspects of Education Policy

Faculty of Education, Bar-Ilan University · Founder and Chair, 2023–present

I founded and chair this forum to bring together researchers and policymakers working on the economic dimensions of education policy in Israel — funding formulae, resource allocation, cost-effectiveness, and the links between education finance and broader social and economic outcomes.

### Annual conferences

- **2024** — Second Annual Scientific Conference: *Education Under Conditions of Uncertainty*, within the Economic Aspects of Education Policy Forum, Bar-Ilan University
- **2023** — Annual Scientific Conference of the Economic Aspects of Education Policy Forum, Bar-Ilan University
