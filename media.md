## Media

Where my research has reached the public — podcast, press, and broadcast. See also: [Policy Impact](policy.html).

### Podcast

- **[Bar Daat](https://www.youtube.com/@bardaat_barilanuniversity)** (בר-דעת), Bar-Ilan University's official podcast — episode: [How much should a teacher earn in Israel?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ySvUtQG7yrU) A discussion of teacher salary policy in Israel and whether higher pay actually attracts and retains stronger teachers.

### Press

- Globes — [Op-ed: market mechanisms don't belong in the education system](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001413696) (May 2022). Argues against importing private-sector market logic into public education.
- Globes — [Op-ed: paying teachers by student results is neither effective nor right](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001402307) (Feb 2022). A critique of pay-for-performance schemes for teachers.
- Ynet — ["The strong get stronger": gaps in local education budgets](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/yokra14865257) (2026). Coverage of research (with Dr. Ruth Meoded) showing wealthier municipalities widening their lead in education spending.
- Ynet — [How much does your municipality invest in your kids' education?](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sjii8rvlel) (May 2025). On the correlation between municipal spending and matriculation (bagrut) exam results.
- Ynet — [Equitable resource allocation and the achievement gap](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5049756,00.html) (Nov 2017). Makes the case for differential budgeting to narrow gaps between rich and poor, Jewish and Arab students.
- Walla News — ["The quiet collapse" of the education system](https://news.walla.co.il/item/3850470) (2026). On teacher shortages and growing inter-municipality inequality, featuring former Minister of Education Yuli Tamir; cites research showing decentralization widens gaps.
- Davar — [The Treasury's decentralization plan is destructive](https://www.davar1.co.il/691705/) (2026). Argues that decentralizing education funding is especially damaging for a country with gaps as wide as Israel's.
- Davar — [Gap-reduction funding is allocated without a research basis](https://www.davar1.co.il/624512/) (2025). On the disconnect between how gap-reduction funds are distributed and what the research actually shows works.
- Yediot Ahronot — [Achievement gap research with Dr. Yossef Klein](https://www.yediot.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-4785454,00.html) (Mar 2016). A Bar-Ilan study finding the achievement gap widened by 15% over a decade despite policy efforts.
- Israel Hayom — [Matriculation exam achievement gap](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/582785) (Aug 2018). An analysis of bagrut results across core subjects (math, English, literature, Tanakh) and what drives the score patterns.
