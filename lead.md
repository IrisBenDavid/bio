## LEAD: Learning for Strategic Development

**ISF International Workshop · Bar-Ilan University · May 5–7, 2025**

In May 2025 I led LEAD, an international workshop funded by the Israel Science Foundation, hosted at Bar-Ilan University's Faculty of Education. The workshop brought together leading scholars in education finance and economics of education to develop the theoretical and empirical grounding of the **Middle-Line Model** — a framework for advancing both state competitiveness and social cohesion through education finance policy, rather than treating them as a trade-off.

### Speakers included

- **Prof. Martin Carnoy**, Stanford University
- **Prof. Bruce D. Baker**, University of Miami
